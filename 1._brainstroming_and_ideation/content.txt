Project Name: PocketSmart AI

Problem Identified:
People often find it difficult to plan their budget for home interiors, parties, and jewellery purchases.

Idea:
Develop an AI-powered assistant that provides budget-based recommendations according to the user's needs and available budget.

Main Features:
• Home Interior Planner
• Party Planner
• Jewellery Planner
• AI-based Recommendations
• User Login and Registration
• Recommendation History
